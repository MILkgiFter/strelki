## Full description (max 4000 chars)
The board is packed with winding arrows. Tap one and it flies away in its direction — if the path is clear. Release every arrow and they form a picture: an elephant, a dragon, the Eiffel Tower or an astronaut.

Simple rules, yet every level makes you think: which arrow goes first so the others don't get stuck?

★ 200 levels — from tiny warm-ups to giant boards with hundreds of arrows
★ 100+ silhouette pictures: animals, fairy-tale heroes, vehicles, world wonders
★ New mechanics along the way: stones, locks and keys
★ Daily level and daily gift — come back every day, day 7 brings a bigger gift
★ Daily tasks and picture collections in your album
★ Themes: light, sunset, neon, chalkboard — plus different arrow styles
★ Boosters: the magnifier shows a move, the eraser removes an arrow, a life gives a second chance
★ Colour-blind mode, relaxing music, plays offline
★ No timer — play at your own pace

Free to play. Optional purchases (no ads, booster packs, looks) and ads that can be removed.
